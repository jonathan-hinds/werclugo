# Working on Wurcluego

Before changing this repository, read [`PROJECT_MANIFEST.md`](./PROJECT_MANIFEST.md). It is the durable product and engineering brief for this project.

The shortest correct interpretation is:

> Wurcluego is a technically reliable, mobile-first clue-collection game whose terminology, procedures, and explanations are intentionally circular and confusing.

Rules for agents:

1. Preserve the distinction between **intentional conceptual confusion** and actual defects. Fix broken controls, unclear system state, inaccessible interactions, data loss, unstable navigation, security mistakes, and unreliable APIs. Do not “fix” the product by making its fictional systems ordinary or fully rational.
2. Keep the application completely sincere. Product copy must never explain the joke, wink at the user, call itself meaningless, or say that it is intentionally confusing.
3. Prefer clean engineering and ridiculous UX—not ridiculous engineering. Domain rules must remain explicit, testable, server-authoritative, and centrally configured.
4. Treat the canonical name as **Wurcluego**, even though the repository/directory may be spelled `werclugo` or `Werclugo`.
5. Do not silently replace functional features with mocks, placeholders, or TODOs.
6. Preserve mobile usability, accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, deep links, persistence, and the pseudo-AR fallback while extending the chaos.
7. Operational decisions explicitly requested by the owner may override older deployment guidance. Keep secrets server-side and never place them in the browser bundle.
